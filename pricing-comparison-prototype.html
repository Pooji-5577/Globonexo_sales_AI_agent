Qualification01

Qualify first. Spend with intent.
Truth boundary02

The model knows what it does not know.

Missing facts are explicit, required facts are a hard gate, and unsupported claims are not a fallback.

Ask the model to “avoid hallucinating.”
Name the gap. Refuse the guess.
Message integrity03

A draft is invalid until it proves otherwise.

Recipient, company, placeholders, formatting, sequence step, and duplicate content are checked before approval.

Human approval is the final safety net.
Validation carries the first load.

Autopilot changes approval, not the checks.

Replies, opt-outs, bookings, and send-time eligibility are re-evaluated before anything goes out.

Remember to stop every follow-up.
The system stops itself.
